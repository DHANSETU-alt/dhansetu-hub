## Objection replies

- "Is this safe with my bank details?" → "We never ask for bank passwords, UPI PINs, or OTPs. You type in amounts yourself — nothing connects to your account."
- "Will it guarantee I save money?" → "No — LeakShield only reviews patterns in what you enter and flags them for you to review. What you do with that is up to you. No guaranteed savings, no financial advice."
- "Why would I pay if there's a free plan?" → "The free plan covers 300 entries a month, which is plenty for most people starting out. SmartBudget Pro is ₹149, paid once — never monthly — and removes the cap plus unlocks full LeakShield. If you also want the Resume AI toolkit, All Access is ₹399 once for both."
- "I already use [Excel/Walnut/another app]." → "Fair — SmartBudget's difference is LeakShield's pattern review, and it's ₹149 once, not a subscription. Happy to show a 2-minute demo if useful."
- "Does it work on my phone?" → "Yes, it's a mobile-friendly web app — no install needed, works in any phone/tablet/desktop browser."
