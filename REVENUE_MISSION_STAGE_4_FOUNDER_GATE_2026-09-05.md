## Recommended Revenue Mission

| Field | Proposal |
|---|---|

| Buyer | Founder, owner, finance manager, or accounts-receivable lead |

| Outcome | Install a relationship-safe system that organizes overdue invoices, sends founder-approved reminders, records promise-to-pay dates, and routes exceptions to a human |
